# Executive Employment Covenant - Restrictive Covenants

## Authoritative Contractual Provisions
Section 6.2 Non-Competition Covenant: During the term of this Agreement and for a period of one (1) year thereafter, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, own, manage, operate, join, or participate in the business of any direct competitor providing enterprise database software within the continental United States without prior written approval.
