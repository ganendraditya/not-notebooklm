# Lithium Superionic Sulfide Electrolytes for All-Solid-State Energy Storage

## Scientific Methodology & Experimental Findings
Among solid electrolyte materials, the sulfide superionic conductor Li10GeP2S12 (LGPS) exhibits room-temperature lithium-ion conductivity of 1.2 x 10^-2 S/cm, rivaling standard organic liquid electrolytes. The three-dimensional interconnected lithium conduction framework facilitates rapid isotropic ion transport with an activation energy of only 0.22 eV.
